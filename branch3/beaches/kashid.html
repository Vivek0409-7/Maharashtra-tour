DISCOVER KASHID

White sand shores nestled along Casuarina forests

Kashid Beach is one of the most popular beach destinations in Maharashtra, located between Alibaug and Murud in Raigad district. It is famous for its clean white sand coastline, gentle waves, and lush green Suru groves.

It serves as an exciting getaway for adventure sports enthusiasts and weekend holidaymakers from Mumbai and Pune.

How to Reach Kashid

Routes and transport guidelines for reaching Kashid.

🚗

By Road

Drive via Alibaug or Roha route from Mumbai (~130 km) or Pune via Mulshi / Kolad (~170 km).

Mumbai / Pune → Alibaug / Roha → Kashid
🚆

By Train

Roha railway station on Konkan Railway is around 35 km away from Kashid Beach.

Roha Station → Taxi / Auto → Kashid
⛴️

By Speedboat

Take Ro-Ro or speedboat ferry from Gateway of India to Mandwa, then drive 45 km to Kashid.

Gateway → Mandwa Jetty → Alibaug → Kashid
✈️

By Air

Mumbai Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Airport is the primary domestic & international airport.

Mumbai Airport → Drive / Ferry → Kashid
