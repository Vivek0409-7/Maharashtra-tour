Bordi Beach
DISCOVER BORDI

A peaceful haven of orchards and shallow tides

Bordi Beach is located in Dahanu taluka in Maharashtra's Palghar district. Known for its extremely shallow sea level where water recedes for half a kilometer during low tide, it offers a safe, peaceful getaway.

Surrounded by extensive Chikoo (Sapota) orchards and Parsi heritage bungalows, Bordi provides a unique blended feel of agri-tourism and coastal leisure.

How to Reach Bordi

Transportation options for visiting Bordi Beach.

🚗

By Road

Drive via NH 48 (Mumbai-Ahmedabad Highway) up to Kasa / Dahanu, then turn towards Bordi.

Mumbai → NH 48 → Dahanu → Bordi
🚆

By Train

Dahanu Road and Gholvad railway stations on Western Railway connect Bordi directly.

Gholvad / Dahanu Road Station → Auto → Bordi
🚌

By Bus

MSRTC buses run regularly from Dahanu and Palghar bus stands to Bordi village.

MSRTC Bus → Dahanu → Bordi
✈️️

By Air

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport in Mumbai is the nearest airport (~150 km).

Mumbai Airport → Train / Drive → Bordi
