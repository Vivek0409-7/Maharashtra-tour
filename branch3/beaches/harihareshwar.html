DISCOVER HARIHARESHWAR

A sacred coastal sanctuary carved by nature

Harihareshwar is a major spiritual and coastal hub located in Raigad district. Surrounded by four hills—Harihareshwar, Harshinagiri, Bramhagiri, and Kalagiri—it is revered as "Dakshin Kashi".

It is famous for its ancient Shiva temple and the unique rocky pradakshina hill path right where crashing waves meet dramatic black rock formations.

PLAN YOUR JOURNEY

How to Reach Harihareshwar

Routes and transport options for visiting Harihareshwar.

🚗

By Road

Drive via NH 66 turning at Mangaon towards Shrivardhan and Harihareshwar (~200 km from Mumbai).

Mumbai / Pune → Mangaon → Harihareshwar
🚆

By Train

Mangaon Railway Station on Konkan Railway network is around 60 km away.

Mangaon Station → Taxi / Bus → Harihareshwar
🚌

By Bus

MSRTC buses operate regularly from Mumbai, Pune, Thane, and Mangaon to Harihareshwar.

Bus → Direct to Harihareshwar Stand
✈️

By Air

Mumbai Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Airport and Pune Airport are closest hubs.

Airport → Drive → Harihareshwar
