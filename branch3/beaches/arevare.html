DISCOVER AREVARE

A scenic coastal curve of tranquility

Arevare Beach, along with its twin sister beach Ware, creates one of the most picturesque coastal drives in the Ratnagiri district. Framed by rolling green hills, this bay offers crystal-clear waters and peaceful solitude.

It remains untouched by heavy commercialization, providing travellers with pure coastal nature, panoramic hilltop views, and gentle shorelines.

WHY AREVARE?

What makes Arevare special?

Arevare stands out for its dramatic geography where cliffs drop down into pristine waters. It offers clean surroundings and undisturbed nature close to Ganpatipule and Ratnagiri city.

PLAN YOUR JOURNEY

How to Reach Arevare

Choose the best route for your journey to Arevare Beach.

🚗

By Road

Drive via the scenic Ratnagiri-Ganpatipule coastal road. Arevare lies around 15 km from Ratnagiri town.

Ratnagiri → Coastal Highway → Arevare
🚆

By Train

Ratnagiri Railway Station is majorly connected on the Konkan Railway network, located 20 km away.

Ratnagiri Junction → Taxi/Auto → Arevare
🚌

By Bus

Take MSRTC buses to Ratnagiri or Ganpatipule, then switch to local transport for Arevare.

MSRTC Bus → Ratnagiri → Arevare
✈️

By Air

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Airport, Mumbai or Goa Airport are the nearest major air terminals.

Airport → Train / Drive → Arevare
