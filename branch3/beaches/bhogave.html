DISCOVER BHOGAVE

Where river currents meet peaceful shores

Bhogave Beach is located in the Sindhudurg district of Southern Maharashtra. It is renowned for its untouched beauty, fine white sand, and unique location at the mouth of the Karli River backwaters.

From Bhogave, visitors can catch stunning views of the historic Nivati Fort perched on nearby cliffs and witness playful dolphins playing off the coast.

How to Reach Bhogave

Routes and directions for reaching Bhogave Beach.

🚗

By Road

Drive via NH 66 (Mumbai-Goa Highway) up to Kudal or Parule, then follow state roads to Bhogave.

NH 66 → Kudal / Parule → Bhogave
🚆

By Train

Kudal railway station on Konkan Railway is the nearest major station, located ~20 km away.

Kudal Station → Taxi/Auto → Bhogave
🚌

By Bus

State transport buses operate between Kudal/Malvan bus stands and nearby Parule village.

Bus → Kudal → Local Auto → Bhogave
✈️

By Air

Chipi Airport (Sindhudurg) is the closest domestic airport, approximately 25 km away.

Chipi Airport → Cab → Bhogave
