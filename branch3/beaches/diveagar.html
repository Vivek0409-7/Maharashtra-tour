DISCOVER DIVEAGAR

A peaceful shoreline lined with betel nut orchards

Diveagar Beach is a famous tourist location in the Shrivardhan taluka of Raigad district. It features a 5-km long stretch of clean, gently sloping coastline bordered by dense Casuarina and betel nut (supari) wadis.

Famous historically for the Golden Ganesha idol temple, Diveagar remains a top pick for family weekend retreats from Mumbai and Pune.

How to Reach Diveagar

Detailed routes to reach Diveagar Beach easily.

🚗

By Road

Drive via Mumbai-Goa Highway (NH 66) via Mangaon or Tamhini Ghat route from Pune (~160 km).

Mumbai / Pune → Mangaon → Diveagar
🚆

By Train

Mangaon railway station on Konkan Railway is around 48 km from Diveagar.

Mangaon Station → Taxi / Bus → Diveagar
🚌

By Bus

Direct MSRTC buses connect Mumbai, Pune, and Thane with Shrivardhan and Diveagar.

MSRTC Bus → Mangaon → Diveagar
✈️

By Air

Pune Airport (~170 km) and Mumbai International Airport (~180 km) are the nearest options.

Airport → Drive → Diveagar
