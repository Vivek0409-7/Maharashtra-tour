Murud Beach
DISCOVER MURUD

A lively coastal hub in the heart of Dapoli

Murud Beach is located in Dapoli taluka in Ratnagiri district. Known for its wide flat shoreline of blackish-golden sand, gentle waves, and rich array of water sports.

It serves as the main hub for beach tourism in Dapoli, providing quick access to dolphin watching boat trips at nearby Harnai port.

How to Reach Murud

Transportation choices and directions for Murud Beach Dapoli.

🚗

By Road

Drive via Khed towards Dapoli town, then continue 10 km down to Murud Beach.

NH 66 → Khed → Dapoli → Murud
🚆

By Train

Khed Railway Station on Konkan Railway network is around 40 km from Murud.

Khed Station → Taxi / Bus → Murud
🚌

By Bus

Regular MSRTC buses travel to Dapoli stand, followed by local auto-rickshaws to Murud.

Bus Stand → Dapoli → Murud
✈️

By Air

Mumbai and Pune Airports are the primary international transit points.

Airport → Train / Drive → Murud
