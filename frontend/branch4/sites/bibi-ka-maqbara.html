DISCOVER MONUMENT

The Taj of the Deccan

Commissioned in 1668 by Prince Azam Shah in memory of his mother Dilras Banu Begum (Posthumously Rabia-ud-Daurani), Bibi Ka Maqbara was designed by Ata-ullah, son of Ustad Ahmad Lahori (chief architect of Agra's Taj Mahal).

Set within a walled Charbagh garden with fountains and reflection pools, the central tomb features white marble detailing and delicate plaster lattice stucco work.

Jali Screen Admiration

Examine the pierced marble screen surround enclosing the octagonal grave chamber below.

📸

Reflecting Pool Photography

Capture symmetrical reflections of the central white marble dome at sunset.

📜

Inscription Reading

Spot the architect’s name 'Ata-ullah' carved near the main entrance wooden gate.

🌿

Mughal Garden Stroll

Relax under shade trees lining the four quadrant garden enclosures.

💡

Night Lights Viewing

Witness the exterior floodlights illuminate the four corner minarets after dusk.

How to Reach Bibi Ka Maqbara

🚗

By Auto / Cab

Located in Begumpura district, easily reached via auto-rickshaw or taxi from anywhere in the city.

City Center → University Road → Begumpura
🚆

By Train

Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar Railway Station is located ~6 km away.

Railway Station → Auto Rickshaw (15 mins)
🚌

By Bus

City transit buses and tourist coaches drop off at the Begumpura monument stop.

Central Bus Stand → City Bus to Maqbara
✈️

By Air

Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar Airport (IXU) is situated ~11 km east.

Airport → Airport Road → Begumpura
