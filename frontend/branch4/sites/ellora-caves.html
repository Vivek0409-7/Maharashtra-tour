DISCOVER ELLORA

A Marvel of Monolithic Rock-Cut Architecture

Carved out of the Charanandri hills between the 6th and 10th century CE, Ellora features 34 major caves representing Hindu (1-12), Buddhist (13-29), and Jain (30-34) traditions coexisting peacefully.

Crown jewel Cave 16—the Kailash Temple—was carved top-down from a single volcanic rock cliff, excavating over 200,000 tons of rock under the Rashtrakuta dynasty.

WHY ELLORA?

What makes Ellora unique?

Ellora demonstrates ancient Indian engineering skill and religious harmony through three distinct faith traditions carved into a single continuous cliff face.

How to Reach Ellora Caves

🚗

By Road

30 km from Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar via smooth four-lane highway connectivity.

Sambhajinagar → Khuldabad Road → Ellora
🚆

By Train

Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar Railway Station is well-connected to major Indian cities (~32 km).

Railway Station → Cab / City Bus
🚌

By Bus

Direct MSRTC buses operate every 15 minutes from Sambhajinagar Central Bus Stand.

Central Bus Stand → Ellora Stop
✈️

By Air

Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar Airport (IXU) is located ~36 km away.

Airport → Highway Cab
