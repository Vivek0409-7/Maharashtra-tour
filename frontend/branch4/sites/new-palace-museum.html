DISCOVER PALACE

Royal Legacy of Kolhapur's Kings

Constructed between 1877 and 1884 under British architect Major Mant, the New Palace served as the primary royal residence of Maharajah Chhatrapati Shahu Maharaj.

Built using polished black basalt stone from local quarries, the ground floor houses the Shahaji Chhatrapati Museum showcasing royal armor, gold swords, stuffed wildlife trophies, and historical photographs.

WHY VISIT NEW PALACE?

Living Royal Residence

The New Palace is unique as it serves as both an active residence for the descendants of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj and a public heritage museum.

How to Reach New Palace

🚗

By Road

Situated directly along Bhausingji Road in northern Kolhapur city.

NH 48 → Pune-Kolhapur Highway → City Center
🚆

By Train

Chhatrapati Shahu Maharaj Terminus (Kolhapur Railway Station) is located ~3 km away.

Railway Station → Auto Rickshaw (10 mins)
🚌

By Bus

Kolhapur Central Bus Stand (CBS) offers direct bus services from Pune, Mumbai, and Goa.

CBS Bus Depot → City Auto to New Palace
✈️

By Air

Kolhapur Airport (KLH) at Urun Islampur road is ~11 km away.

Airport → Airport Road → City Center
