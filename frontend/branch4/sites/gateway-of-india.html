DISCOVER GATEWAY

The Grand Ceremonial Entrance to India

Erected in yellow basalt stone between 1911 and 1924, the Gateway of India combines Roman triumph arch grandeur with intricate 16th-century Gujarati Islamic architectural detailing.

Standing at Apollo Bunder overlooking the Mumbai Harbour, it served as the formal ceremonial entrance for British Viceroys and ironically marked the departure point of the last British troops in 1948.

How to Reach Gateway of India

🚗

By Taxi / Cab

Easily accessible via BEST buses, black-and-yellow kaali-peeli taxis, or ride apps from anywhere in Mumbai.

Marine Drive / CSMT → Colaba → Apollo Bunder
🚆

By Train

Churchgate (Western) and CSMT (Central) railway stations are located within 2.5 km.

CSMT / Churchgate → Taxi to Gateway
🚌

By Bus

Multiple direct BEST bus routes terminate at Electric House, Colaba bus depot.

Electric House Depot → 5 Min Walk
✈️

By Air

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport (BOM) is ~25 km north via Coastal Road / Eastern Freeway.

Airport → Freeway → South Mumbai
