DISCOVER CSMT

Where Victorian Grandeur Meets Indian Artistry

Designed by British architect F. W. Stevens and opened in 1888 as Victoria Terminus, CSMT is a UNESCO World Heritage site that serves as the headquarters of India's Central Railway.

Featuring a stone dome, ribbed vaults, carved gargoyles, stained glass windows, and peacock motifs, it remains one of the world's busiest functional railway terminals.

How to Reach CSMT

🚆

By Central Railway

CSMT is the primary terminus station for Central Local lines and long-distance express trains.

Central Local Trains → Platform 1-18
🚗

By Taxi

Kaali-Peeli and app taxis drop off passengers directly at the main heritage concourse gate.

Freeway / Marine Drive → Fort Junction
🚌

By Bus

Dozens of BEST bus routes stop right outside CSMT on Dr. D.N. Road.

CSMT Bus Depot / Bhatia Baug Stop
✈️

By Air

23 km from Mumbai International Airport via the Eastern Freeway connection.

Airport → Eastern Freeway → CSMT
