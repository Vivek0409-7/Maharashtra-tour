Overview

Murud-Janjira Fort is a legendary marine stronghold standing proudly on an oval-shaped island rock in the Arabian Sea, right off the coastal village of Murud in Raigad district. Recognized globally as one of the most impregnable sea fortifications in human history, Janjira remained completely undefeated throughout centuries of naval warfare against the Marathas, Portuguese, and British Empire.

Originally erected in the 15th century by Koli fishermen as a wooden fortress, it was substantially fortified by the Siddi dynasty with 19 rounded bastions and towering 40-foot granite walls that rise directly out of the ocean surf. Remarkably, despite being completely enveloped by saltwater, the fortress contains large freshwater lakes that supplied fresh drinking water to its garrison.

Historical Highlights

The Undefeated Sea Citadel

Neither Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj nor Sambhaji Maharaj or the Portuguese could break through Janjira's defenses. Sambhaji Maharaj even attempted to build a stone causeway across the sea from nearby Padmadurg.

The Great Kalal Bangadi Cannons

Janjira boasted heavy artillery including the legendary Kalal Bangadi, Landakasam, and Chavri bronze cannons—renowned for their immense range, heat resistance, and sheer destructive maritime power.

Freshwater Miracles at Sea

Inside the fort complex lie two large freshwater lakes (Sheshai Talav), deep natural sweet water cisterns that sustained hundreds of inhabitants during extended months of maritime blockade.

Major Attractions

Attraction Description
Daryi Darwaza (Hidden Sea Entrance) Concealed sea-level entrance designed so it remains invisible until boats are within yards.
19 Mighty Rounded Bastions Imposing monolithic stone bastions surviving centuries of pounding ocean waves.
Kalal Bangadi & Historic Cannons Third largest cannon in India, forged from a rare alloy of five metals.
Surul Khan's Palace Ruins Atmospheric multi-storey ruins of the royal Siddi residence and administrative court.
Sweet Water Reservoirs (Sheshai Talav) Two large freshwater lakes flourishing in the midst of the Arabian Sea.
Ancient Mosque & Royal Bathhouses Architectural remains reflecting Persian, Islamic, and Konkani design influences.

Essential Visitor Information

  • Category: Fort
  • Location: Raigad District, Arabian Sea Coast, Maharashtra
  • Elevation: Sea Level (Island Fortress surrounded by Arabian Sea)
  • Best Time to Visit: October to March (Calm seas & pleasant breezes)
  • Entry Timings: 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM (Subject to ocean tides)
