Overview

Lonavala and Khandala are the twin hill resorts of the Sahyadri mountains, perched gracefully at an elevation of 625 metres along the Mumbai-Pune corridor. Blessed with lush cascading green valleys, mist-shrouded peaks, and roaring monsoon waterfalls, they are the most accessible and popular hill getaways in Western India.

Beyond their natural beauty and the famous sweet confection called Chikki, the region holds rich 2,000-year-old historical treasures. Here lie the magnificent rock-cut Buddhist chaitya complexes of Karla Caves and Bhaja Caves, alongside Maratha hill forts like Rajmachi, Visapur, and Lohagad.

Historical Highlights

2,000-Year-Old Buddhist Sanctuaries

Carved during the 2nd century BCE, Karla Caves boast the largest and best-preserved rock-cut Buddhist Chaitya hall in India, featuring soaring teak wood ribbed ceilings and intricately carved elephant pillars.

The Bhor Ghat Railway Wonder

The challenging Bhor Ghat connecting Mumbai to the Deccan Plateau was a milestone engineering feat opened in 1863, conquering 25 tunnels and dramatic mountain inclines.

Monsoon Tourism & Chikki Heritage

Every monsoon, tens of thousands flock to Bhushi Dam's overflowing steps and waterfalls, while the heritage bazaars offer dozens of varieties of crunchy nut chikki and fudge.

Major Attractions

Attraction Description
Tiger's Leap & Lion's Point Sheer cliff lookouts with 650m drop-offs delivering wind blasts and cloud sweeps.
Bhushi Dam & Pavana Dam Famous overflow steps and vast scenic backwaters for camping and water sports.
Karla Caves & Bhaja Caves Ancient 2nd-century BCE rock-hewn Buddhist shrines with grand stupas and monasteries.
Duke's Nose (Nagphani Cliff) Pointing rock cliff resembling the Duke of Wellington's nose, popular for rappelling.
Kune Falls & Kataldhar Falls Stunning multi-tiered 200-metre cascades plunging down the Khandala valley.
Lonavala Bazaar & Chikki Emporiums Vibrant town center offering authentic peanut, cashew, and rose petal chikki.

Essential Visitor Information
