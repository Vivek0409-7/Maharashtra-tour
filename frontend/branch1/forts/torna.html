Overview

Torna Fort, historically known as Prachandagad (meaning 'The Colossal Fort'), holds an immortal place in Indian history as the first fort captured by Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj in 1646 when he was just 16 years old. This triumphant capture heralded the dawn of the Maratha Empire and the struggle for Hindavi Swarajya.

Located in the Velhe taluka of Pune district, Torna is the highest hill fortress in Pune district, soaring to 1,403 metres above sea level. Renowned for its enormous ridges—including the razor-sharp Zunjar Machi and the massive Budhla Machi—Torna offers sweeping views of Rajgad, Sinhagad, Raigad, and the sparkling backwaters of Bhatghar Dam.

Historical Highlights

The First Victory at Age 16 (1646)

With a band of loyal Mavlas, the youthful Shivaji captured Torna without shedding blood, establishing his sovereign flag and uncovering hidden gold jars that funded the construction of Rajgad.

The Daring Zunjar Machi

Zunjar Machi is a long, knife-edge rocky promontory fortified with high stone walls and rock-cut watch posts, creating a natural mountain bastion that dominated the western valley routes.

Spiritual Guardians of Torna

Ancient shrines dedicated to Toranjai Devi and Menghai Devi crown the central plateau, offering safe shelter to trekkers and preserving centuries of rural folklore.

Major Attractions

Attraction Description
Zunjar Machi A daring, razor-thin fortified ridge requiring caution, surrounded by 1,000-foot drop-offs on both sides.
Budhla Machi A unique rocky formation resembling an overturned oil pot ('Budhla'), boasting strong double ramparts.
Menghai Devi Temple The spiritual sanctum at the center of the plateau offering panoramic sunrise views.
Bini Darwaza & Kothi Darwaza The rugged stone gateway entrances fortified with deep bastion curves.
Kokankada of Torna A colossal cliff face providing dramatic downward views into the misty Konkan river basin.
Bhatghar Dam Panorama Unobstructed vistas of the gleaming reservoir waters framed against green mountain chains.

Essential Visitor Information

  • Category: Fort
  • Location: Pune District (near Velhe), Maharashtra
  • Elevation: 1,403 metres (4,603 ft) above sea level
  • Best Time to Visit: September to February (Post-monsoon lushness & cool winters)
  • Entry Timings: 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM
