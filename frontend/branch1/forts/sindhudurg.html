Overview

Sindhudurg Fort (meaning 'Ocean Fort') is the magnum opus of Maratha naval engineering, constructed between 1664 and 1667 on the Kurte island rock off the Malvan shoreline in southern Konkan. Built under the direct visionary guidance of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj, this 48-acre island stronghold served as the premier headquarters of the Maratha Navy, countering Portuguese, British, and Siddi maritime supremacy.

The fortress is enclosed by a massive 3-kilometre perimeter wall reinforced with 42 grand bastions. Over 4,000 mounds of iron were used for casting, and foundation stones were laid in molten lead to withstand pounding Arabian Sea breakers. Sindhudurg is also uniquely revered for housing the only temple where Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj is worshipped as a deity, complete with his authenticated footprint and handprint impressions.

Historical Highlights

The Birth of Maratha Sea Power (1664)

Recognizing that 'He who rules the sea rules the land', Shivaji Maharaj personally chose Kurte Island and engaged master architects Hiroji Indulkar to construct an invincible naval garrison.

Shri Shivchhatrapati Temple

Constructed by Rajaram Maharaj in 1695, this sacred sanctum features a black stone idol of Shivaji Maharaj portrayed in a seated boat-rowing posture without a beard, honoring his maritime identity.

Royal Footprint & Handprint Impressions

Protected under glass domes on the ramparts are the authentic stone impressions of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj's left hand and footprint left in wet lime mortar during construction.

Major Attractions

Attraction Description
Shri Shivchhatrapati Temple The world's only historical temple dedicated to Shivaji Maharaj as the presiding deity.
Handprint and Footprint Impressions Sacred rock impressions left by Shivaji Maharaj preserved on the fortress parapet.
Doodh Bav, Sakhar Bav & Dahi Bav Three miraculously fresh, sweet drinking water wells amidst the salty ocean.
42 Ocean Bastions & 3 km Rampart Walk Elevated stone walkway offering breathtaking views of crashing ocean waves.
Concealed Sea Main Entrance (Dilli Darwaza) A masterfully hidden gateway invisible from approaching enemy vessels.
Malvan Coral Reefs & Scuba Zone Vibrant underwater marine life and diving centers located directly around the fort.

Essential Visitor Information

  • Category: Fort
  • Location: Malvan, Sindhudurg District, Konkan Coast, Maharashtra
  • Elevation: Sea Level (Island Fortress surrounded by the Arabian Sea)
  • Best Time to Visit: October to March (Gentle sea breeze & optimal water clarity)
  • Entry Timings: 8:00 AM to 5:30 PM (Ferry operates subject to weather)
