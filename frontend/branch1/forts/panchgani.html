Overview

Panchgani is a picturesque hill retreat nestled amidst five towering hills of the Sahyadris at an altitude of 1,293 metres in Satara district, located just 18 km before Mahabaleshwar. Renowned for its salubrious year-round climate and pristine oxygen-rich air, Panchgani was developed in the 1860s by British officer John Chesson as a sanatorium and boarding school haven.

Panchgani is universally famous for Table Land, a sprawling 95-acre laterite rock volcanic plateau that is the second longest mountain plateau in Asia. Offering horse rides, paragliding launches, organic strawberry orchards, and sweeping views of the Krishna River valley, Panchgani is one of Western India's most charming destinations.

Historical Highlights

Asia's 2nd Longest Plateau

Table Land is an expansive flat laterite mountain top extending over 95 acres. Created by ancient volcanic lava outflows, it offers wide horizon views and has featured in dozens of iconic Bollywood movies.

Sydney Point & Dhom Dam

Named after Sir Sidney Beckwarth, this elevated promontory delivers sweeping vistas overlooking the vast turquoise reservoir waters of Dhom Dam and the ancient hill fortress of Pandavgad.

Heritage Sanatorium & Schools

Panchgani's pure air attracted Parsi settlers and educators, leading to prestigious residential institutions like St. Peter's and Sanjeewan Vidyalaya that have nurtured leaders for a century.

Major Attractions

Attraction Description
Table Land Plateau Sprawling 95-acre volcanic plateau offering horseback riding, caves, and sweeping sunsets.
Sydney Point Spectacular cliff viewpoint looking down upon Dhom Dam and lush Kamalgad valley.
Parsi Point Scenic overlook perched above the Krishna River valley with lush picnic spots and gardens.
Devil's Kitchen Mythological laterite cave complex believed to have housed the Pandavas during their exile.
Devrai Art Village Non-profit arts commune where tribal craftsmen produce exquisite Dokra brass and iron artwork.
Strawberry & Mulberry Orchards Interactive farm visits offering fresh berry picking and organic fruit jams.

Essential Visitor Information

  • Category: Hill
  • Location: Satara District, Western Ghats, Maharashtra
  • Elevation: 1,293 metres (4,242 ft) above sea level
  • Best Time to Visit: September to May (Crisp mountain air & berry blossoms)
  • Entry Timings: Open 24 Hours (Points open 6:00 AM to 6:30 PM)
