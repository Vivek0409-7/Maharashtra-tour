Overview

Matheran (meaning 'Forest on the Forehead') is Asia's only completely automobile-free hill station, designated as an Eco-Sensitive Zone by the Government of India. Perched in the Raigad district at an elevation of 800 metres, no private motor vehicles are permitted past Dasturi Naka, keeping its air free of exhaust pollution and its forested red-laterite pathways entirely serene.

Visitors explore Matheran on foot, on horseback, or by hand-pulled rickshaws. The journey is made unforgettable by the historic Neral-Matheran Toy Train, a narrow-gauge railway opened in 1907 that snakes up the mountainside. With 38 designated cliff viewpoints overlooking deep Sahyadri gorges, Matheran is an eco-tourism haven.

Historical Highlights

Discovered by Hugh Malet (1850)

The then-Collector of Thane, Hugh Poyntz Malet, discovered Matheran's pristine plateau during a hiking stroll in 1850. Governor Lord Elphinstone subsequently developed it into a peaceful health sanatorium.

The Heritage Mountain Toy Train

Financed by philanthropist Sir Adamjee Peerbhoy in 1907, the 20-kilometre narrow-gauge railway climbs 2,600 feet across Horseshoe Embankment and One Kiss Tunnel.

The 38 Panoramic Viewpoints

From Panorama Point's 360-degree vista to Louisa Point and Charlotte Lake, Matheran's laterite cliff edges offer unobstructed views of Prabalgad, Kalavantin Durg, and Mumbai's horizon.

Major Attractions

Attraction Description
Charlotte Lake Pristine mountain water reservoir surrounded by dense forest, housing the ancient Pisarnath Temple.
Panorama Point A 360-degree vantage point offering stunning sunrises and views of the Western Ghats range.
Echo Point & Louisa Point Cliff edges famous for natural sound echoes and dramatic views of Prabal fort ruins.
One Tree Hill Point A dramatic precipitous hilltop featuring a single lonely jambul tree standing on the edge.
Neral-Matheran Toy Train Ride A heritage 2-hour rail journey navigating steep curves and misty mountain forests.
Mall Road & Leather Craft Markets Quaint vehicle-free town center famous for handmade Kolhapuri leather footwear and honey.

Essential Visitor Information

  • Category: Hill
  • Location: Raigad District, Western Ghats, Maharashtra
  • Elevation: 800 metres (2,625 ft) above sea level
  • Best Time to Visit: October to May (Pure red-soil trails; Lush waterfalls post-monsoon)
  • Entry Timings: Open 24 Hours (No motor vehicles permitted inside town)
