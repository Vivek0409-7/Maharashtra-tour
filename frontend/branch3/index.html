COASTAL MAHARASHTRA

Where the Sahyadri meets the Arabian Sea

Maharashtra's Konkan coastline is home to beautiful beaches, fishing villages, forts, temples and coastal culture. From the peaceful shores of Bordi to the turquoise waters of Tarkarli, every destination offers a different experience.

Plan your coastal journey with Aagman and discover beaches across Palghar, Raigad, Ratnagiri and Sindhudurg.
