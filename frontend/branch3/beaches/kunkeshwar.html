DISCOVER KUNKESHWAR

The Kashi of South Konkan on the ocean edge

Kunkeshwar Beach is located near Devgad taluka in Sindhudurg district. It is famous for its magnificent 11th-century Chola-style Shiva temple built right along the shoreline.

Known affectionately as the "Kashi of South Konkan", it provides travellers with clean sands, coconut trees, and profound spiritual peace.

How to Reach Kunkeshwar

Directions and transportation routes to reach Kunkeshwar.

🚗

By Road

Drive via Tarela or Nandgaon on NH 66 towards Devgad and turn towards Kunkeshwar.

NH 66 → Devgad Road → Kunkeshwar
🚆

By Train

Nandgaon Road (~42 km) or Kankavli Railway Station (~50 km) on Konkan Railway.

Kankavli Station → Taxi / Bus → Kunkeshwar
🚌

By Bus

MSRTC operates buses from Devgad, Kankavli, Mumbai, and Pune to Kunkeshwar temple.

Bus → Devgad → Kunkeshwar Stand
✈️

By Air

Chipi Airport Sindhudurg (~75 km) is the closest domestic airport connection.

Chipi Airport → Cab → Kunkeshwar
