DISCOVER BHANDARPULE

A peaceful haven of waves and suru trees

Bhandarpule Beach is located barely 3 km from Ganpatipule. It features a stunning long stretch of soft sands lined by towering Suru (Casuarina) trees and hillocks on both sides.

It is the ideal quiet alternative for tourists visiting Ganpatipule who want to escape crowd hustle while enjoying powerful ocean waves and natural tranquility.

PLAN YOUR JOURNEY

How to Reach Bhandarpule

Routes and connections to reach Bhandarpule Beach.

🚗

By Road

Drive via Ratnagiri or Niwali on the Mumbai-Goa Highway towards Ganpatipule. Bhandarpule is 3 km before Ganpatipule town.

Niwali / Ratnagiri → Ganpatipule Road → Bhandarpule
🚆

By Train

Ratnagiri railway station is around 28 km away and offers taxis and auto-rickshaws directly to Bhandarpule.

Ratnagiri Station → Taxi/Auto → Bhandarpule
🚌

By Bus

Frequent MSRTC buses connect Ratnagiri to Ganpatipule. Get off at Bhandarpule junction.

Bus → Ganpatipule Bus Stand → Bhandarpule
✈️

By Air

Mumbai and Goa airports are the primary transit hubs for national and international travellers.

Airport → Train / Drive → Bhandarpule
