Nivati Beach
DISCOVER NIVATI

A hidden gem of sea rocks and fort ruins

Nivati Beach is located in the Sindhudurg district between Malvan and Vengurla. It is famous for its isolated sandy cove, colorful fishing boats, and the historic cliffside Nivati Fort overlooking the ocean.

Off the coast lie the famous Nivati Rocks (3D islets) offering a truly rugged and untouched natural coastal experience.

How to Reach Nivati

Routes and transit details to reach Nivati Beach.

🚗

By Road

Drive via NH 66 turning at Kudal or Parule towards Nivati coast line.

NH 66 → Kudal → Parule → Nivati
🚆

By Train

Kudal Railway Station on Konkan Railway network is located ~25 km away.

Kudal Station → Taxi / Auto → Nivati
🚌

By Bus

MSRTC buses travel to Parule or Vengurla, followed by local auto rickshaws to Nivati.

Bus → Kudal / Vengurla → Nivati
✈️

By Air

Chipi Airport Sindhudurg is around 25 km from Nivati Beach.

Chipi Airport → Cab → Nivati
