DISCOVER GUHAGAR

A tranquil white-sand paradise between two rivers

Guhagar Beach is a beautiful coastal town located between the Vashishti River and Jaigad Creek in Ratnagiri district. Known for its soft, pristine white sand, calm tides, and dense coconut plantations.

It remains one of the cleanest and most serene family beaches in Central Maharashtra with a rich cultural heritage.

How to Reach Guhagar

Transportation options and travel routes for Guhagar.

🚗

By Road

Drive via Chiplun from NH 66 (Mumbai-Goa Highway). Guhagar is 45 km west of Chiplun town.

NH 66 → Chiplun → Guhagar Road → Guhagar
🚆

By Train

Chiplun Railway Station on Konkan Railway connects directly to Mumbai, Pune, and Goa.

Chiplun Station → Taxi/Bus → Guhagar
🚌

By Bus

MSRTC buses operate frequent direct trips to Guhagar from Chiplun, Mumbai, and Pune.

Bus Stand → Direct Bus → Guhagar
✈️

By Air

Mumbai and Goa airports serve international and domestic air visitors.

Airport → Train / Drive → Guhagar
