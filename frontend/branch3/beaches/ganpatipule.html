DISCOVER GANPATIPULE

A holy coastal oasis of peace and devotion

Ganpatipule Beach is one of Maharashtra's most celebrated coastal destinations, located in the Ratnagiri district. It is famous for its 400-year-old self-manifested Swayambhu Ganesha temple situated right on the golden shore.

The town combines sacred spiritual traditions with clean stretches of sand, water sports, and beautiful hillside coastal drives.

How to Reach Ganpatipule

Routes and directions for reaching Ganpatipule Beach.

🚗

By Road

Drive via NH 66 turning at Niwali towards Ganpatipule (~375 km from Mumbai / 330 km from Pune).

Niwali → Ganpatipule Road → Ganpatipule
🚆

By Train

Ratnagiri railway station on Konkan Railway network is 30 km from Ganpatipule.

Ratnagiri Junction → Auto/Taxi → Ganpatipule
🚌

By Bus

Regular MSRTC and private luxury buses run from Mumbai, Pune, and Kolhapur directly.

Direct Bus → Ganpatipule Stand
✈️

By Air

Ratnagiri Airport or Goa / Mumbai Airports are useful transit stops.

Airport → Drive → Ganpatipule
