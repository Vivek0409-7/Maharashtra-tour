DISCOVER AJANTA

A Masterpiece of Classical Ancient Buddhist Art

Carved into the steep face of a horseshoe-shaped gorge along the Waghora River between the 2nd century BCE and the 6th century CE, Ajanta houses 30 rock-cut caves that preserved classical Buddhist art.

The murals portray Jataka tales, celestial beings, and royal life using natural pigments that have endured for millennia.

WHY VISIT AJANTA?

What makes Ajanta world famous?

Ajanta represents the golden age of Indian art, featuring unmatched rock-cut architecture alongside tempera paintings preserved for over two thousand years in dense natural forest surroundings.

How to Reach Ajanta Caves

🚗

By Road

Located ~100 km from Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar along state highways with frequent taxi services.

Sambhajinagar → Jalgaon Road → Ajanta
🚆

By Train

Jalgaon Railway Station (60 km) and Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (100 km) are the closest main rail hubs.

Jalgaon / Sambhajinagar Station → Bus / Taxi
🚌

By Bus

Regular MSRTC government buses connect Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar and Jalgaon to Ajanta Caves T-Junction.

Central Bus Stand → Ajanta Shuttle Hub
✈️

By Air

Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar Airport (IXU) is the closest airport connection (~98 km).

Airport → Cab to Ajanta
