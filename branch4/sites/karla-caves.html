DISCOVER KARLA

India's Largest Ancient Rock Prayer Hall

Carved into a high rocky hill off the ancient trade route connecting the Arabian Sea ports to the Deccan interior, Karla Caves date back to the 2nd century BCE under the Satavahana dynasty.

The main Chaitya hall measures over 37 meters long and features carved pillars topped with elephant riders, as well as original 2,000-year-old teak wood arches attached to the vaulted stone roof.

THINGS TO DO

Cave Highlights

🏛️

Chaityagriha Exploration

Walk down the central aisle flanked by massive octagonal pillars carved with elephant capitals.

🪵

Ancient Timber Viewing

Look up at the intact 2,000-year-old teak wood ribbing attached to the arched stone ceiling.

🦁

Ashokan Lion Pillar

Examine the free-standing stone pillar near the entrance topped with four carved lions.

🥾

Hill Staircase Climb

Climb the paved stone hill path offering sweeping views of the Western Ghats valley.

⛩️

Ekvira Devi Temple

Visit the revered temple located right outside the main cave entrance plaza.

📸

Facade Screen Photography

Capture photos of the grand horseshoe arched window (Sun Window) that lights up the inner hall.

WHY VISIT KARLA?

Pinnacle of Early Cave Art

Karla represents the absolute peak of Satavahana period rock-cut architecture in Western India.

✓

Largest single rock-cut Buddhist prayer hall in South Asia

✓

Original ancient timber roof beams preserved for over two millennia

✓

Intricate Mithuna (couple) sculptures flanking the main entrance screen

✓

Located close to Mumbai-Pune Expressway and Lonavala hill station

How to Reach Karla Caves

🚗

By Road

Located right off the Old Mumbai-Pune Highway (NH 48), ~11 km from Lonavala town center.

Mumbai / Pune → NH 48 → Karla Phata
🚆

By Train

Malavli station is 3 km away (local trains). Lonavala station is 11 km away for express trains.

Lonavala / Malavli → Auto Rickshaw to Hill Base
🚌

By Bus

State transport buses stop at Karla Phata on the Old Mumbai-Pune Highway.

Karla Phata Stop → Local Auto to Base
✈️

By Air

Pune International Airport (PNQ) is ~60 km away via expressway.

Pune Airport → Cab to Lonavala / Karla
