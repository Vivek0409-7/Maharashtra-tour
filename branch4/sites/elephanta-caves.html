DISCOVER ELEPHANTA

Rock-Cut Sanctuaries in the Arabian Sea

Located on Gharapuri Island in Mumbai harbour, the Elephanta Caves date back to the 5th and 7th centuries CE. Cave 1 displays high-relief basalt sculptures illustrating Shaivite mythology.

The centerpiece is the 6-meter-tall Maheshamurti (Trimurti) carving portraying Lord Shiva in three forms: Creator, Preserver, and Destroyer.

Highlights & Experiences

Trimurti Viewing

Enjoy a scenic 1-hour ferry ride from Gateway of India past ships and sea birds.

🚂

Toy Train Ride

Ride the mini electric train running along the jetty pier from the boat landing to the hill steps.

🥾

Cannon Hill Trek

Climb to the island's summit to see two old Portuguese defense cannons overlooking the bay.

Island Market Street

Browse stone items, wooden crafts, and marble souvenirs along the staircase shops.

📸

Basalt Relief Walk

Examine carved panels portraying Gangadhara, Ardhanarishvara, and Ravana shaking Mount Kailash.

WHY VISIT ELEPHANTA?

How to Reach Elephanta Caves

🚢

By Ferry

Regular boats depart every 30 minutes from Gateway of India Jetty (9 AM – 3:30 PM).

Gateway Jetty → 1 Hour Boat → Elephanta
🚂

Island Toy Train

Available at the island pier to transport visitors across the long concrete jetty.

Jetty Pier → Toy Train → Steps Base
🥾

By Stairs

120 paved steps lead from the jetty base up to Cave 1 entrance plaza.

Base Market → 120 Steps → Cave Entrance
✈️

By Air

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Airport (BOM) → Taxi to Gateway of India Jetty.

Airport → South Mumbai → Gateway Ferry
