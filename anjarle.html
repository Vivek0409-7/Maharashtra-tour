DISCOVER ANJARLE

A quiet corner of the Konkan coast

Anjarle Beach is located in Dapoli taluka in Maharashtra's Ratnagiri district. It is known for its clean sandy coastline, coconut groves, gentle hills and peaceful atmosphere.

Unlike heavily commercialised beaches, Anjarle offers a more relaxed Konkan experience with traditional villages, local food and beautiful coastal scenery.

WHY ANJARLE?

What makes Anjarle special?

Anjarle combines natural beauty with traditional Konkan culture. The beach is particularly known for its peaceful surroundings, coconut and areca plantations, sunsets and nearby religious sites.

PLAN YOUR JOURNEY

How to Reach Anjarle

Choose the transport option that suits your trip.

🚗

By Road

Anjarle is approximately 20 km from Dapoli. Visitors from Mumbai and Pune can travel towards Dapoli and continue by local road towards Anjarle.

Mumbai / Pune → Dapoli → Anjarle
🚆

By Train

Khed is an important railway connection for the Dapoli region. From the railway station, continue towards Dapoli and then Anjarle by road.

Khed → Dapoli → Anjarle
🚌

By Bus

State transport and private road services can be used to reach Dapoli, followed by local transport towards Anjarle.

Bus → Dapoli → Local transport
✈️

By Air

Travellers arriving by air can use a suitable airport connection and continue the journey towards Dapoli and Anjarle by road.

Airport → Dapoli → Anjarle
